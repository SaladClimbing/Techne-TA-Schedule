FAQ

Do I need to book a time? Not for any hours listed above. Feel free to book time with me if you would like 1 on 1 time.

I can’t make any of your hours. What now? Email me with 2–3 windows that work for you and I’ll find a slot, or post your question on the course forum.

Can I come with a group? Yes — group work is encouraged.

Will you do my homework for me? No, but I’ll happily talk through the concepts, point you to the right notes, and review your approach.

How do I get updates when hours change? This page is the source of truth. Changes are announced in class and reflected here.

Schedule subject to change during the semester. If something on this page looks out of date, email me and I’ll fix it.

Questions? See the Contact section above.
